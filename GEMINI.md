# A1 Gallery

- A1 Gallery is a hand-curated gallery of 1,150+ real websites, with captured sections, full pages and fonts.
- Its design values are measured off the live page: hex colours with roles, type sizes and weights, spacing, radius and layout.
- It is useful when you need real design reference, such as examples of a hero, pricing section or font pairing.
- 19 of the 21 tools are read-only. save_to_board and remove_from_board change only the signed-in person’s own boards; ask before saving. Calls need an A1 account: free accounts get 50 calls a day, Pro gets 2,000.
- Docs: https://www.a1.gallery/mcp
